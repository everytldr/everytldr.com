# About everytldr

everytldr helps readers follow news originally written in languages they do not read.

The service collects non-Korean source articles from external publishers, generates AI summaries in supported languages, classifies each article into a product-defined category, and links back to the original source. The goal is not to replace original reporting. everytldr is designed to help readers decide what deserves deeper reading.

## What we provide

- AI-generated summaries in Korean and English
- Article lists and detail pages for quick scanning
- Source links and publisher attribution for every article
- Lightweight anonymous discussion through likes and threaded comments

## How to read summaries

Summaries are brief by design. They may omit nuance, background, later corrections, or updates from the original source. For important decisions, always open and read the linked source article.

## Editorial approach

everytldr stores and displays summaries and article metadata, not full copyrighted article bodies. Summaries should preserve the source article's core facts, attribution, and topic context while staying concise and easy to scan.
